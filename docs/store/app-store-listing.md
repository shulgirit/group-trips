Together is the trip planner for groups: families, friends, couples, anyone traveling with other people. Everything the group needs lives in one shared place, on everyone's phone.

PLAN TOGETHER
• A shared day-by-day schedule everyone can see and edit
• Save places and ideas, with photos, opening hours and notes
• An interactive map of your whole trip
• Polls, so the group decides instead of arguing in a chat

YOUR AI TRAVEL GUIDE
• Ask for recommendations, a rainy-day plan or a full day itinerary
• It adds places to your list and events to the schedule for you
• Answers start in seconds and speak your language

SPLIT EXPENSES
• Log who paid for what, in any currency
• See who owes whom and settle up at the end

FOR REAL GROUPS
• Invite people with a link, no account needed to look around
• Families and kids are supported, with simple roles for each person
• Reminders before events
• Documents and bookings in one place: tickets, reservations, passports
• Available in English, Hebrew, Arabic and Spanish

Your private chats stay private. Only what you choose to save is shared with the group.

Free to use. Sign in with Google or an email code.

## App Privacy (nutrition label) — "Data not used to track you"
Collected, linked to the user, for app functionality only (never advertising, never tracking):
- Contact info: email address, name
- User content: photos, files/documents, other user content (places, schedule, chat messages)
- Location: precise location, only if the user turns on live location sharing in a trip
- Identifiers: user ID, device push token
- Usage data / diagnostics: none collected by third-party analytics
Third parties that process data on our behalf: Convex (hosting), OpenAI (AI guide text), Google (Maps/Places), Resend (sign-in emails), Expo (push delivery).
